Terms, Privacy & Policy Center

AniCool was founded on a simple premise: a clean, responsive anime and manga discovery portal with zero advertisements, zero invasive scripts, and complete respect for user privacy. Here is the full legal and technical explanation of how the platform operates.

block 1. The Zero-Advertisement Guarantee

Many streaming catalogs and wiki aggregators degrade user experience with intrusive video popunders, redirects, malicious ad-networks, and paywalls. AniCool explicitly rejects this model:

  • check_circle Zero Commercial Advertisements: We will never place banner ads, video interludes, sponsored popups, or interstitial redirects across any page of this site.
  • check_circle Zero Data Monetization: We do not track, profile, package, rent, or sell your reading habits, viewing queues, or browsing actions to any advertising broker or analytics firm.
  • check_circle Community Sustained: The domain, development, and hosting costs are supported independently through direct voluntary donations from patrons.
database 2. Local Storage & Zero-Log Architecture

AniCool runs entirely as a static client-side web application hosted via GitHub Pages. We deliberately avoid maintaining proprietary database servers that log or retain personal user profiles.

info Where is my watchlist saved?
Your saved anime, manga lists, favorites, and history entries live exclusively inside your browser's local window.localStorage memory on your own physical computer or phone.
  • check_circle Browser Isolation: Because your library is stored locally, clearing your browser cache or site data will remove your local watchlist unless you export a JSON backup beforehand.
  • check_circle Zero Tracking Cookies: AniCool does not plant persistent tracking cookies, fingerprinting canvases, or cross-site tracking beacons onto your machine.
api 3. Third-Party API Architecture (AniList)

AniCool interfaces dynamically with public endpoints provided by the AniList GraphQL API (v2) to fetch poster art, episode counts, character casts, studio credits, and airing countdown schedules:

  • check_circle OAuth2 Authorization: When you connect your AniList account, authentication is completed via AniList's official OAuth2 Implicit Grant protocol. Your secret token is held solely in your local browser storage.
  • check_circle Independent Project: AniCool is an open community dashboard and is not officially associated with, sponsored by, or operated by AniList.co.
  • check_circle API Compliance: All read and write operations conform to standard AniList rate limits and terms of service.
gavel 4. DMCA & Copyright Disclaimer

AniCool respects intellectual property rights and complies with the provisions of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA):

  • check_circle No Hosted Video or Comic Files: AniCool does not operate video streaming servers, file storage lockers, or manga scanlation hosts. We do not copy, upload, convert, or encode copyrighted media.
  • check_circle Informational Index: All titles, character likenesses, descriptions, cover posters, and trademarks displayed belong to their respective copyright holders, Japanese production committees, studios, and authorized publishers.
mail Filing a Notice
If you are a copyright owner and believe metadata or indexing on AniCool infringes your rights, please provide formal identification of the work and contact our administrative team through our Discord community portal.
rule 5. Acceptable Platform Use

By utilizing AniCool, you agree not to misuse or disrupt the platform or its connected infrastructure. Users must not:

  • close Attempt to flood or DDoS the platform or overwhelm the public AniList GraphQL endpoints through automated query spam.
  • close Attempt to bypass adult content filters to expose non-consenting users to NSFW media.
  • close Reverse-engineer or re-bundle the project to inject ad-ware, mining scripts, or telemetry loggers.
forum 6. Community Hub & Discord Conduct

The AniCool official Discord guild is a welcoming place for anime enthusiasts, manga readers, and developers. All members are expected to follow basic community etiquette:

  • favorite Mutual Respect: Zero tolerance for hate speech, targeted harassment, racism, threats, or discriminatory behavior.
  • visibility_off Spoiler Tags: Always mark plot twists, series endings, and upcoming manga events with spoiler tags to respect other viewers.
  • warning NSFW Boundaries: Explicit 18+ content must strictly stay contained inside designated age-restricted channels.
