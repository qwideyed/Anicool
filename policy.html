Privacy Policy & Terms

AniCool is committed to an open, ad-free, and tracker-free anime and manga discovery platform. Here is how we handle data, user privacy, and third-party content.

shield 1. Zero-Ad & No Tracking Guarantee

AniCool does not serve third-party advertisements, popup windows, paywalls, or commercial tracker scripts. We do not sell, license, or monetize any user activity, habits, or browsing behavior.

database 2. Local Data & Storage

AniCool is built on client-side web technologies and does not operate centralized database servers storing your personal lists:

  • check_circle Watchlist & History: Saved entries, favorites, and watch progression are stored locally on your device in your web browser's localStorage.
  • check_circle AniList Synchronization: When connecting your AniList account, your authentication token is stored only in your local browser storage to communicate directly with the official AniList GraphQL API.
api 3. Third-Party Content & APIs

Media catalog information, metadata, voice actors, posters, and release schedules displayed across AniCool are dynamically requested from public endpoints provided by the AniList API. AniCool is an independent open-web client and is not affiliated with or endorsed by AniList.co.

gavel 4. DMCA & Copyright Disclaimer

AniCool does not host, upload, or store video files, copyrighted anime episodes, or comic media files on any server. All titles, posters, synopses, and imagery belong to their respective copyright holders and publishers. AniCool functions solely as an informational index and personal discovery tool.
